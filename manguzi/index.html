// ENGINEERING INSIGHTS

Challenges & Solutions

BRAND IDENTITY

Premium Hospitality Aesthetic

Challenge: Creating a website that conveys the elegance and executive quality of the lodge while remaining functional and fast-loading.

Solution: Leveraged Abril Fatface display typography paired with Inter for body text, combined with a refined color palette and generous whitespace to establish a premium brand presence.

USER EXPERIENCE

Multi-Service Navigation

Challenge: Presenting three distinct services (accommodation, restaurant, conference) without overwhelming visitors or creating navigation confusion.

Solution: Designed a clear multi-page architecture with consistent navigation, dedicated service pages, and prominent "Book Now" CTAs to guide users through the booking journey.

DISCOVERABILITY

Location Accessibility

Challenge: Helping potential guests find the lodge's physical location in Karonga, especially for first-time visitors unfamiliar with the area.

Solution: Integrated an embedded Google Maps widget directly into the contact section, allowing visitors to view the exact location and get directions without leaving the site.
