The Brief

Lilongwe Adventist Hospital required reliable visual coverage of key indoor and outdoor areas, with footage retained on-site and accessible to authorized personnel. Bits & Bytes Computers contracted the deployment; I served on the installation team supporting deployment and configuration through commissioning and handover.

$ ./deploy.sh --scope

What Was Installed

COVERAGE

13x 6MP Cameras

Installed and aimed 13 high-resolution 6MP cameras — 6 outdoor units covering perimeters and entry points, 7 indoor units covering corridors and service areas.

RECORDING

32-Channel NVR + 2TB Storage

Installed and configured a 32-channel Network Video Recorder with a 2TB surveillance-grade hard drive for continuous on-site footage retention, with channel headroom for future additions.

POWER

Dedicated UPS Backup

Connected the NVR directly to a dedicated UPS so recording continues through mains outages — no gaps in footage when hospital power drops.

ACCESS

Secure Remote Viewing

Configured authenticated remote access so authorized hospital personnel can monitor live cameras and review recorded footage from mobile devices or computers.

$ tail -f handover.log

Result & Handover

The system was installed, configured, tested, and commissioned successfully, then formally handed over to Lilongwe Adventist Hospital for operational use — with an operation & maintenance guide covering:

  • Power reliability for the NVR, UPS, and network equipment.
  • Verifying cameras stay online and recording.
  • Monitoring NVR storage and exporting footage before overwrite.
  • Maintaining stable internet connectivity for remote viewing.
  • Restricting system access to authorized personnel only.
  • Periodic UPS battery checks.
  • A fault-reporting process for issues and support requests.
$ tail -f lessons.log

Field Notes

COVERAGE

Balancing Indoor and Outdoor Coverage

Entry points, perimeters, corridors, and service areas each needed a different mounting height and viewing angle. Camera positions were walked and verified on live view before final fixing.

POWER

Outage-Proof Recording

Hospital mains power is not guaranteed. The NVR runs on a dedicated UPS so recording continues through outages — verified by simulating a mains cut during commissioning.

HANDOVER

Leaving the Client Self-Sufficient

The operation & maintenance guide covers storage monitoring, footage export before overwrite, access control, UPS checks, and fault reporting — so the system stays healthy after handover.
